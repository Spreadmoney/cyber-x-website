Your limits grow as you verify more about yourself. These limits are set by the Central Bank of Nigeria.

- Tier 1 :: Your NIN and a selfie. Send up to ₦50,000 a day and keep up to ₦300,000.
- Tier 2 :: Add your BVN. Send up to ₦200,000 a day and keep up to ₦500,000.
- Tier 3 :: Add an ID document and proof of address. Send up to ₦5,000,000 a day, with no balance limit.

To move up, open Profile in the app and choose Raise my limits.
