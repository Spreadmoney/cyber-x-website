Paying someone on Cyber X follows the Central Bank of Nigeria’s 2026 Guide to Charges.

- Up to ₦5,000 :: Free
- Up to ₦50,000 :: ₦10
- Above ₦50,000 :: ₦50

Airtime and data are free, bills are ₦50 each, and Pay me safely is free for buyers. Work out any fee on [Fees and limits](/fees-and-limits).
