---
title: What happens to payments while I’m frozen?
summary: Money in still arrives. Nothing leaves.
topic: security
updated: 2026-11-14
related: how-do-i-freeze-my-account, how-do-i-unfreeze-my-account, why-is-my-payment-waiting
---

While your account is frozen, money coming in still arrives, so people can still pay you. Nothing leaves your account, and nobody can sign in.

- Payments you were sending :: Stop until you unfreeze.
- Payments waiting to be accepted :: Stay waiting. If they aren’t accepted in 72 hours, they come back to you as usual.
- Online payment numbers :: Charges are declined while you’re frozen.
- Pay me safely orders :: Money already held stays held until the order is settled.
