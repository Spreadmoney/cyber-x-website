Unfreezing needs a short video check, so only you can turn your account back on.

1. Sign in on a phone you trust. :: Use the Cyber X app on the phone you’ll keep using.
2. Start the video check. :: The app shows you how. It takes a few minutes with a person on our team.
3. Choose a new PIN. :: If anyone might know your old one, change it before you pay again.

Until then, money coming in still arrives. Nothing leaves.
