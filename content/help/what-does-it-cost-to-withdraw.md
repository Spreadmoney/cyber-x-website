Withdrawing to your bank costs the same as a transfer, following the Central Bank of Nigeria’s 2026 Guide to Charges.

- Up to ₦5,000 :: Free
- Up to ₦50,000 :: ₦10
- Above ₦50,000 :: ₦50

The fee and the total show on the review screen before you approve. See every fee on [Fees and limits](/fees-and-limits).
