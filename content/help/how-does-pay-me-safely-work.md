When you buy from someone you don’t know, Pay me safely holds your money until your order arrives.

1. You pay. Cyber X holds it. :: It doesn’t reach the seller yet.
2. The seller sends your order. :: They can see your money is secured.
3. You confirm it arrived. :: Then the seller is paid.

No Cyber X account? You can pay a Pay me safely link by card. It’s free for buyers.
