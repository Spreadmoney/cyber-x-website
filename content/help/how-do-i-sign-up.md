You need to be 18 or older, live in Nigeria and have a NIN.

1. Get the app. :: From the App Store or Google Play. See [Get the app](/get-the-app).
2. Verify who you are. :: Your NIN and a live selfie.
3. Choose your alias. :: People pay you with it. You can change it later in Profile.
4. Sign in with biometrics. :: Face ID or your fingerprint, with a password as a backup.
