title: Can I withdraw to someone else’s account?
summary: No. You can only withdraw to an account in your name. To pay someone, use their alias.
topic: add-and-withdraw
updated: 2026-11-11
related: how-do-i-withdraw-to-my-bank, whats-an-alias, how-do-i-add-money

No. Withdrawals only go to a bank account in your own name. It keeps your money safe if someone else gets into your phone.

To pay another person, use their alias or Cyber X ID, so you see who they are before you approve.
