- Bank transfer :: Free, from any Nigerian bank, to your own Cyber X account number.
- Card :: Scan your card instead of typing. A 0.5% fee.
- Cash at an agent :: Hand cash to a partner agent near you. It goes straight into your account.

Your account number is only for adding your own money. People pay you by alias.
