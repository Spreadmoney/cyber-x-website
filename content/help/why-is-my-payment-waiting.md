When you pay someone for the first time, the money waits until they accept it. Until then, it’s still yours.

- Take it back :: Changed your mind? Take it back from the payment in the app.
- Or let it return :: If it isn’t accepted in 72 hours, it comes back by itself, with nothing extra to pay.

Money from someone new waits for your yes too, in Settlements.
