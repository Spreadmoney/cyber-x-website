title: How do I ask someone for money?
summary: Send a request to someone you’ve both saved, or share your alias.
topic: paying
updated: 2026-11-13
related: why-is-my-payment-waiting, how-do-i-take-back-a-payment, whats-an-alias
---

You set the amount and the reason, and the other person approves it.

- Request money :: Send a request to a mutual: someone you’ve both saved. Strangers can’t send you requests.
- Split a bill :: Pick the people and the shares. Everyone gets a request.
- Share your alias or QR :: Anyone can pay you with it. Your account number stays private.
