Passwords are reset in the Cyber X app, on the phone your account uses. It keeps a stranger with your number from changing it.

1. Open Cyber X on your phone. :: Tap Sign in, then Use your password instead.
2. Tap Forgot password? :: We text a code to your number.
3. Take a selfie, then choose a new password. :: Every other phone and browser is signed out.

No phone, or a new one? [Freeze your account](/freeze) first if you need to, then [get the app](/get-the-app) on the new phone and sign in. We'll guide you through it.
