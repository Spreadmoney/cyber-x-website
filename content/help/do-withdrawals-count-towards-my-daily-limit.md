title: Do withdrawals count towards my daily limit?
summary: Yes. Withdrawals share your daily send limit.
topic: fees-and-limits

Yes. Withdrawals share your daily send limit, so what you withdraw and what you send add up together.

Your tier sets the limit. To move more each day, [raise your limits](/help/how-do-i-raise-my-limits).
