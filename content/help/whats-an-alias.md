An alias is a short name, like @kemi.fabrics, that people use to pay you. It’s checked, so when someone pays your alias, they see your verified name before they approve.

You can change your alias later in Profile. Your Cyber X ID, like CX-8MKA-4F6R-J9TB, never changes and works too.

> Never pay a phone number or an account number from a stranger. That’s how fraud gets in.
