A payment to someone new waits until they accept it. Until then, you can take it back.

1. Open the payment in the app. :: It shows as Waiting to be accepted.
2. Tap Take it back, then approve.

Taking it back costs the same small fee as sending, and the send fee isn’t returned. If you’d rather wait, a payment that isn’t accepted in 72 hours comes back by itself, with nothing extra to pay.
