Freezing stops money leaving your account and signs out every phone. Money coming in still arrives.

## On this website

1. Go to [Freeze my account](/freeze).
2. Sign in. :: Use your Cyber X ID, alias or phone number with your password, a passkey, or a code by text.
3. Tap Freeze my account, then confirm.

## With no phone at all

Dial the Cyber X USSD code from any phone and choose Freeze.

## Unfreezing

Sign in on a phone you trust and do a short video check with our team.

> ! Shared your PIN or a code? Freeze first, then report it.
