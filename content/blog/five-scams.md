Most scams don’t break into your account. They talk you into paying. Here are the five we see most, and what Cyber X does about each one.

## 1. “Our account number has changed.”

A seller or supplier sends new bank details, often by email or WhatsApp. On Cyber X you pay an alias, so a new account number from a stranger changes nothing.

## 2. Fake credit alerts

Someone shows you a text or a screenshot saying they’ve paid. Texts can be faked. Check the payment in your app, or on our [Check a payment](/check) page, before you hand anything over.

## 3. “Send me the code.”

A caller says they’re from your bank or from Cyber X and asks for a code. Nobody from Cyber X will ever ask for your PIN, password or codes.

## 4. “I sent it to you by mistake.”

Money arrives, then a message asks you to send it back to a different account. Use Return this payment in the app, so it goes back the way it came.

## 5. Fake Cyber X support

A page or account pretends to be us. We only contact you in the app. If you’re not sure, report it.

> If something feels wrong, stop. You can always check a person or a payment first.
