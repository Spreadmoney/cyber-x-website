The Central Bank of Nigeria’s 2026 Guide to Charges came into force on 1 May 2026. It sets the most a bank or payment company can charge for an electronic transfer. Cyber X follows it.

## What a transfer costs

- Up to ₦5,000 :: Free
- ₦5,000.01 to ₦50,000 :: ₦10
- Above ₦50,000 :: ₦50

These are the fees for paying someone on Cyber X and for sending money to your bank.

## What else to know

Adding money by bank transfer is free. Adding money by card costs 0.5% of the amount. Bills are ₦50 each, and airtime and data are free.

Government charges, such as stamp duty, are set by law. When one applies, you see it on the review screen before you approve.
