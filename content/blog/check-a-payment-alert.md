Fake credit alerts are one of the commonest scams we see. An alert is just a message: anyone can type one, and screenshots are easy to edit. Before you hand over goods or cash, take thirty seconds to check.

## The 30-second check

1. Open the Cyber X app. :: A real payment shows in your history, with the payer’s verified name.
2. No app to hand? :: Use [Check a payment](/check) on our website, with the reference and the amount.
3. Still not sure? :: Don’t hand anything over. Ask the buyer to wait while you check.

## Signs it’s fake

- The alert came by text or WhatsApp, not in your app.
- The buyer is rushing you, or says the money will “reflect later”.
- The amount or reference doesn’t match what you agreed.

> Your app is the only place a payment is real. A text, a screenshot or a call never is.
