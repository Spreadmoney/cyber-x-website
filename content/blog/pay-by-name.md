Ten digits say nothing about the person who owns them. That is why so much fraud starts with “send it to this account”. You can’t see who is behind the number until the money has gone.

## A name you can check

On Cyber X you pay an alias, like @kemi.fabrics, or a Cyber X ID. Before you approve, you see the verified name behind it, how long the account has been open, and whether anyone has reported it.

## Your account number stays yours

You still have an account number, but only for adding your own money. People pay your alias instead, so you never have to hand your number to a stranger.

## Why it matters

When a payment knows who it is for, a scammer can’t quietly swap in their own details. Changing an alias doesn’t change who you’re paying, and a new account number in a message changes nothing at all.

That is the idea Cyber X is built on: money should reach the right person, every time.
