X is the intelligence inside Cyber X. It answers from your own account, in plain words, so you can understand your money without digging through a statement.

## What X does today

- Answers questions about a payment, your limits, or how something works.
- Sends a weekly summary: money in, money out, and what you kept.
- Gives you a heads-up when a bill or rent is coming due, or a charge failed.
- Helps you set goals, and reminds you. It never blocks a payment.

## What X will never do

X never moves money on its own. It can fill in a payment for you to check, but nothing happens until you approve it. X doesn’t give investment advice, and your conversations stay yours until you delete them.

When X can’t help, it hands you to a person on our team.

## Where X is going

First it answers. Then it suggests and you approve. In the long run, we want X to help you plan around how you earn and spend, so you keep more of your money.
