## Transcript

“When you buy from someone you don’t know, the hardest part is trusting that the order will come. With Pay me safely, your money waits with Cyber X until it does.

You open the seller’s link and pay. The seller sees Payment secured, so they know the money is there, but they can’t touch it yet.

When the order arrives, you tap It arrived, and the seller is paid. If it never comes, your money goes back to you after the deadline.

And if something’s wrong with the order, tell us before you confirm. We’ll ask you both for evidence and decide.”
