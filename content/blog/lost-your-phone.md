Lost your phone, or think someone knows your PIN? This short film shows how to freeze your account from any browser, and what happens next.

## In this video

- 0:00 :: Freezing on this website
- 0:35 :: What stops, and what still works
- 1:05 :: Freezing with no phone at all
- 1:25 :: Unfreezing

## Transcript

“If your phone is lost or stolen, freeze your account first. Go to Freeze my account on the Cyber X website, sign in, and confirm.

Freezing stops money leaving your account and signs out every phone. Money coming in still arrives.

No phone at all? Dial the Cyber X USSD code from any phone and choose Freeze.

When you’re ready, sign in on a phone you trust and do a short video check with our team to unfreeze.”
